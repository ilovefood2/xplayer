Keep your own videos organized and ready to watch with xplayer.

IMPORT YOUR LIBRARY
Choose videos or entire folders from Files, including supported file providers. Folder imports preserve nested folders and create local copies for offline playback.

ORGANIZE BY FOLDER
Create folders, move videos, and rename folders. You can also transfer videos directly to xplayer with Finder or the Files app; reopen the app or pull to refresh after the transfer.

BUILD YOUR PLAYLISTS
Combine videos from different folders into named playlists. Reorder or remove entries and play videos in sequence with previous and next controls.

FAVORITES AND QUICK CLEANUP
Mark videos as favorites and play them from the Favorites tab. Select multiple files or folders and confirm deletion of their local copies.

OPEN NETWORK VIDEOS
Play direct HTTP or HTTPS video links and HLS streams. Network playback requires a reachable server and supported codecs. Website pages, SMB shares, and DRM streams are not supported. Links are not saved; the server receives your IP address and requested URL. Prefer HTTPS.

WATCH AT YOUR PACE
Double-tap the left or right side of the video to skip back or forward 15 seconds. Tap the speed button and move the slider to choose 0.25× to 2× playback. Seeking depends on the video's available timeline.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, ads, or tracking SDKs. Your videos and playlists are stored locally and are not uploaded to the developer.

SUPPORTED FORMATS
MP4, M4V, and MOV use Apple playback, typically H.264 or HEVC. MKV (Matroska), AVI, WebM, and MPEG-TS (.ts, .mts, .m2ts) use the bundled MobileVLCKit decoder, with play/pause and seeking. Support depends on the encoded media; damaged files or unsupported codecs may not play. DRM-protected media is not supported. You provide your own video files; the app does not include a streaming catalog or download videos from websites.
