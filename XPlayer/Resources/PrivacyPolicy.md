# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

## Your video library

xplayer plays local videos and network links you choose. It copies the video files and folders you
choose into its own storage on your device. Video names, folder paths, and
playlist order, and favorites are saved locally so the app can organize and play your files.
The app does not upload these files or library details to the developer.

## Data collection

xplayer has no account system, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK, or
developer-operated server. It does not collect data from the app for the
developer. It does not access your contacts, location, microphone, or camera.
It only accesses external files or folders you select through the system
Files picker or explicitly open with the app.

## Network playback

When you enter a network video URL and tap Play, xplayer connects to that
server and may follow its redirects to stream the media. The server receives
your IP address and requested URL, including any access token in the link,
and handles these requests under its own privacy policy. HTTP links are
unencrypted; prefer HTTPS. Local-server playback may prompt for Local Network
permission. The app does not scan for servers. Network links are not saved in
your library or a playback history; the system may temporarily buffer media.

## Media decoding

MKV, AVI, WebM, and MPEG-TS playback use the bundled VideoLAN MobileVLCKit library. Other network media
may also use it when native playback cannot open the link. It decodes media on
your device; xplayer does not add analytics, tracking, or a developer-operated
media service. Network playback connects to the media source you choose as
explained above. Decoder license and source information are in About.

## File providers and backups

If you choose a file in iCloud Drive or another provider, that provider may
download it before xplayer can copy it. Your device's backup settings may
include xplayer's local files. File providers, Apple backups, and any system
diagnostics you choose to share are governed by their providers' policies.

## Keeping and deleting data

Your imported files, playlists, and favorites remain on your device until you delete them
or delete the app. Long-press a file or folder in Library to delete the local
copy, or tap Select to delete multiple items after confirmation. Removing a
favorite keeps the video. Deleting a playlist removes its list of entries but keeps the videos.
Deleting a local imported copy does not delete the file at its original
source. Removing the app through iOS deletes its local app data; offloading an
app may preserve data, according to iOS settings. Backups can have separate
retention and deletion controls.

## Support and links

Opening the support website or submitting a support request is optional.
GitHub hosts the website and issue tracker and processes requests under its
own privacy policy. A public support issue is visible to others. Do not include
private videos, filenames, account credentials, or other sensitive information.
The app never attaches your videos or library data to a support request.

If you choose to email a@ikel.ca, the maintainer receives your email address,
message, and any attachments you choose to send. That information is used to
respond to your request and retained only as needed for support or applicable
legal obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by email.

## Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date.
Contact the maintainer at a@ikel.ca or through https://github.com/ilovefood2/xplayer/issues.
